<geometry>
Goal: Draw a square with a triangle attached to its right side.
Canvas/scale: 500x500 pixels.
Main shapes:
- Square with side length 100 pixels.
- Triangle with base and height both 100 pixels, pointing to the right.
Drawing order:
1. Draw the square.
2. Draw the triangle on top of the square.
</geometry>

```scala
clear()
setSpeed(fast)

// Draw the square
forward(100)
right(90)
forward(100)
right(90)
forward(100)
right(90)
forward(100)
right(90)

// Move to the starting point of the triangle
penUp()
forward(50)
left(90)
forward(50)
penDown()

// Draw the triangle
forward(100)
right(120)
forward(100)
```
