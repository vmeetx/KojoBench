<geometry>
Goal: Draw two equal triangles sharing a corner.
Canvas/scale: 500x500 pixels.
Main shapes:
- Two equilateral triangles, each with side length 100 pixels.
Drawing order:
1. Draw the left triangle starting from (0, 0).
2. Move to the shared corner at (50, 0) and draw the right triangle.
</geometry>

```scala
clear()
setSpeed(fast)

// Draw the left triangle
forward(100)
right(120)

// Move to the shared corner and draw the right triangle
penUp()
forward(50)
left(90)

penDown()

forward(100)
right(120)
```
