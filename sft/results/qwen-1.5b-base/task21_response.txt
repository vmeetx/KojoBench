<geometry>
Goal: Draw a semicircle with a flat diameter line at the bottom and the curved arc rising above it.
Canvas/scale: 500x500
Main shapes:
- Circle with radius 250
- Line segment from center to bottom of circle
Drawing order:
1. Draw the circle
2. Draw the line segment
3. Draw the curved arc
</geometry>

```scala
clear()
setSpeed(fast)

// Draw the circle
circle(250)

// Draw the line segment from center to bottom of circle
lineTo(0, -250)

// Draw the curved arc
right(180)
arc(250, 180)
```
