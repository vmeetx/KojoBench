<geometry>
Goal: Draw a square inscribed in a circle.
Canvas/scale: Canvas size is 500x500 pixels.
Main shapes:
- Circle with radius 250
- Square with side length 250
Drawing order:
1. Move to center of circle
2. Draw circle
3. Move to bottom-left corner of square
4. Draw square
5. Move back to center of circle
</geometry>

```scala
clear()
setSpeed(fast)

// Move to center of circle
moveTo(250, 250)

// Draw circle
circle(250)

// Move to bottom-left corner of square
moveTo(225, 225)

// Draw square
square(250)

// Move back to center of circle
moveTo(250, 250)
```
