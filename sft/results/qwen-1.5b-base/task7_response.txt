<geometry>
Goal: Draw a square with a triangle attached to its right side, sharing the same edge where they meet and are the same size.
Canvas/scale: 500x500
Main shapes:
- Square
- Triangle
Drawing order:
1. Square
2. Triangle
</geometry>

```scala
clear()
setSpeed(fast)

// Draw the square
forward(100)
right(90)
forward(100)
right(90)
forward(100)
right(90)
forward(100)

// Draw the triangle
right(120)
forward(50)
right(120)

right(120)
```
