<geometry>
Goal: Draw a triangle with two semicircles attached to its base.
Canvas/scale: 500x500
Main shapes:
- Triangle
- Two semicircles
Drawing order:
1. Draw the triangle
2. Draw the first semicircle
3. Draw the second semicircle
</geometry>

```scala
clear()
setSpeed(fast)

// Draw the triangle
forward(100)
right(120)
forward(100)
right(120)
forward(100)

// Draw the first semicircle
right(90)
forward(50)
left(90)
forward(50)

// Draw the second semicircle
right(90)
forward(50)
left(90)
forward(50)
```
