<geometry>
Goal: Draw a large equilateral triangle with a smaller one inside it.
Canvas/scale: 500x500 pixels.
Main shapes:
- Large equilateral triangle with side length 300 pixels.
- Small equilateral triangle with side length 150 pixels, centered within the large triangle.
Drawing order:
1. Draw the large equilateral triangle.
2. Move to the center of the large triangle.
3. Draw the small equilateral triangle.
</geometry>

```scala
clear()
setSpeed(fast)

// Draw the large equilateral triangle
val sideLengthLarge = 300
repeat(3) {
  forward(sideLengthLarge)
  right(120)
}

// Move to the center of the large triangle
penUp()
forward(sideLengthLarge / 2)
left(60)
forward(sideLengthLarge * sqrt(3) / 4)
penDown()

// Draw the small equilateral triangle
val sideLengthSmall = 150
repeat(3) {
  forward(sideLengthSmall)
  right(120)
}
```
