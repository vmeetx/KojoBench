<geometry>
Goal: Draw a right-angled isosceles triangle.
Canvas/scale: 500x500 pixels.
Main shapes:
- Two equal-length sides forming a right angle.
- One diagonal side connecting the endpoints of the other two sides.
Drawing order:
1. Move forward to create one side.
2. Turn right by 90 degrees and move forward to create the second side.
3. Turn right by 90 degrees again and move forward to complete the triangle.
</geometry>

```scala
clear()
setSpeed(fast)

forward(100)
right(90)
forward(100)
right(90)
forward(141.42) // Length of the diagonal side (sqrt(100^2 + 100^2))
```
