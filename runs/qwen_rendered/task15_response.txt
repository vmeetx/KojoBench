<geometry>
Goal: Draw a shape made of two rectangles joined together at a shared corner in the lower left.
Canvas/scale: 500x500 pixels.
Main shapes:
- Tall narrow rectangle (height 200, width 50) on the left side.
- Wide short rectangle (width 150, height 50) along the bottom.
Drawing order:
1. Draw the tall narrow rectangle.
2. Move to the shared corner and draw the wide short rectangle.
</geometry>

```scala
clear()
setSpeed(fast)

// Draw the tall narrow rectangle on the left side
forward(50)
right(90)
forward(200)
right(90)
forward(50)
right(90)
forward(200)
right(90)

// Move to the shared corner and draw the wide short rectangle along the bottom
penUp()
forward(50)
left(90)
forward(50)
penDown()
forward(150)
right(90)
forward(50)
```
