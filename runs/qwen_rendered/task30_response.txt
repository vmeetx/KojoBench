<geometry>
Goal: Draw a square with one of its diagonals from top right to bottom left.
Canvas/scale: 500x500 pixels.
Main shapes:
- Square with side length 200 pixels.
- Diagonal line from (100, 400) to (300, 100).
Drawing order:
1. Draw the square.
2. Draw the diagonal.
</geometry>

```scala
clear()
setSpeed(fast)

// Draw the square
forward(200)
right(90)
forward(200)
right(90)
forward(200)
right(90)
forward(200)

// Move to the starting point of the diagonal
penUp()
setPosition(100, 400)
penDown()

// Draw the diagonal
lineTo(300, 100)
```
