<geometry>
Goal: Draw a square with one of its diagonals from top left to bottom right.
Canvas/scale: 500x500 pixels.
Main shapes:
- Square with side length 200 pixels.
- Diagonal line from (0, 200) to (200, 0).
Drawing order:
1. Draw the square.
2. Draw the diagonal.
</geometry>

```scala
clear()
setSpeed(fast)

// Draw the square
forward(200)
right(90)
forward(200)
right(90)
forward(200)
right(90)
forward(200)

// Move to the top left corner
penUp()
setPosition(0, 200)
penDown()

// Draw the diagonal from top left to bottom right
lineTo(200, 0)
```
