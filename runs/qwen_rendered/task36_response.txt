<geometry>
Goal: Draw two circles touching at one point.
Canvas/scale: 500x500 pixels.
Main shapes:
- Larger circle with radius 100 pixels.
- Smaller circle with radius 50 pixels.
Drawing order:
1. Draw the larger circle to the left.
2. Move right by the sum of the radii and draw the smaller circle to the right.
</geometry>

```scala
clear()
setSpeed(fast)

// Draw the larger circle to the left
right(360, 100) // Position the turtle at the center of the larger circle

// Draw the smaller circle to the right
hop(150) // Move right by the sum of the radii (100 + 50)
right(360, 50) // Position the turtle at the center of the smaller circle
```
