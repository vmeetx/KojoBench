<geometry>
Goal: Draw a shape with two rectangles.
Canvas/scale: 500x500 pixels.
Main shapes:
- Wide horizontal rectangle (100x20)
- Narrow vertical rectangle (40x100)
Drawing order:
1. Draw the wide horizontal rectangle.
2. Move to the center of the bottom edge of the wide rectangle.
3. Draw the narrow vertical rectangle.
</geometry>

```scala
clear()
setSpeed(fast)

// Draw the wide horizontal rectangle
forward(100)
right(90)
forward(20)
right(90)
forward(100)
right(90)
forward(20)
right(90)

// Move to the center of the bottom edge of the wide rectangle
penUp()
forward(50)
left(90)
forward(10)
penDown()

// Draw the narrow vertical rectangle
forward(40)
right(90)
forward(100)
right(90)
forward(40)
right(90)
```
