<geometry>
Goal: Draw an "S" shape.
Canvas/scale: 500x500 pixels.
Main shapes:
- Top half of the "S": curve to the right.
- Bottom half of the "S": curve to the left.
- Straight line connecting the top and bottom halves.
Drawing order:
1. Draw the top half of the "S".
2. Draw the straight line.
3. Draw the bottom half of the "S".
</geometry>

```scala
clear()
setSpeed(fast)

// Top half of the "S"
forward(50)
right(90, 50)

left(90, 50)

penUp()
forward(200)
penDown()
```
