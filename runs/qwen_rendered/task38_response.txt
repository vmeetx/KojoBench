<geometry>

Canvas/scale: 500x500 pixels.
Main shapes:
- Circle 1: Radius 50, centered at (250, 450)
- Circle 2: Radius 75, centered at (350, 450)
- Circle 3: Radius 100, centered at (450, 450)
Drawing order:
1. Draw the first circle.
2. Draw the second circle.
3. Draw the third circle.
</geometry>

clear()
setSpeed(fast)

// Draw the first circle
penUp()

penUp()

penDown()
