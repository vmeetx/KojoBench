<geometry>
Goal: Draw an arrow pointing to the right.
Canvas/scale: 500x500 pixels.
Main shapes:
- Rectangle for the body.
- Triangle for the head.
Drawing order:
1. Draw the rectangle.
2. Draw the triangle on top of the rectangle.
</geometry>

```scala
clear()
setSpeed(fast)

// Draw the rectangular body of the arrow
forward(150)
right(90)
forward(50)
right(90)
forward(150)
right(90)
forward(50)

// Move to the top right corner of the rectangle to draw the triangle head
penUp()
forward(75)
left(45)
penDown()

// Draw the triangular head
forward(106.066) // Length of the hypotenuse of a 45-45-90 triangle with legs of length 50
right(90)
forward(106.066)
```
