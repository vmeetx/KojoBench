<geometry>
Goal: Draw two triangles side by side, both pointing up. They share one corner at the bottom in the middle.
Canvas/scale: 500x500 pixels
Main shapes:
- Two isosceles triangles with a base of 100 pixels and height of 86.6 pixels.
Drawing order:
1. Draw the first triangle on the left.
2. Move to the shared corner.
3. Draw the second triangle on the right.
</geometry>

```scala
clear()
setSpeed(fast)

// Draw the first triangle on the left
forward(50)
right(75)
forward(86.6)
left(150)
forward(86.6)
left(75)
back(50)

// Move to the shared corner
penUp()
forward(50)
penDown()

// Draw the second triangle on the right
forward(50)
right(75)
forward(86.6)
left(150)
forward(86.6)
left(75)
back(50)
```
