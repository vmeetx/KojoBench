- Four diagonal lines
Drawing order:

2. Draw the first diagonal line from the top left to the bottom right.
3. Draw the second diagonal line from the top right to the bottom left.
4. Draw the third diagonal line from the bottom left to the top right.
5. Draw the fourth diagonal line from the bottom right to the top left.

</geometry>
