<geometry>
Goal: Draw two squares, one centered inside the other with equal gaps on all sides.
Canvas/scale: 500x500 pixels.
Main shapes:
- Outer square with side length 400 pixels.
- Inner square with side length 300 pixels.
Drawing order:
1. Draw the outer square.
2. Move to the center of the outer square.
3. Draw the inner square.
</geometry>

```scala
clear()
setSpeed(fast)

// Draw the outer square
forward(400)
right(90)
forward(400)
right(90)
forward(400)
right(90)
forward(400)
right(90)

// Move to the center of the outer square
penUp()
forward(50)
left(90)
forward(50)
penDown()

// Draw the inner square
forward(300)
right(90)
forward(300)
right(90)
forward(300)
right(90)
forward(300)
```
