<geometry>
Goal: Draw four triangles of different sizes in a row.
Canvas/scale: 500x500 pixels.
Main shapes:
- Triangle 1: Side length 50
- Triangle 2: Side length 75
- Triangle 3: Side length 100
- Triangle 4: Side length 125
Drawing order:
1. Draw Triangle 1 at position (0, 0).
2. Move to the right of Triangle 1 and draw Triangle 2.
3. Move to the right of Triangle 2 and draw Triangle 3.
4. Move to the right of Triangle 3 and draw Triangle 4.
</geometry>

```scala
clear()
setSpeed(fast)

// Draw Triangle 1
forward(50)
right(120)
forward(50)
right(120)
forward(50)
right(120)

// Move to the right of Triangle 1 and draw Triangle 2
hop(75)
forward(75)
right(120)
forward(75)
right(120)
forward(75)
right(120)

// Move to the right of Triangle 2 and draw Triangle 3
hop(100)
forward(100)
right(120)
forward(100)
right(120)
forward(100)
right(120)

// Move to the right of Triangle 3 and draw Triangle 4
hop(125)
forward(125)
right(120)
forward(125)
right(120)
forward(125)
right(120)
```
