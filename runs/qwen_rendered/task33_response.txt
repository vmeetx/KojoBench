<geometry>
Goal: Draw two overlapping squares.
Canvas/scale: 500x500 pixels.
Main shapes:
- Square 1 with side length 200 pixels.
- Square 2 with side length 200 pixels, offset to overlap with Square 1.
Drawing order:
1. Draw Square 1.
2. Draw Square 2 overlapping with Square 1.
</geometry>

```scala
clear()
setSpeed(fast)

// Draw the first square
forward(200)
right(90)
forward(200)
right(90)
forward(200)
right(90)
forward(200)
right(90)

// Move to the starting position for the second square
penUp()
forward(100)
left(90)
forward(50)
penDown()

// Draw the overlapping square
forward(200)
right(90)
forward(200)
right(90)
forward(200)
right(90)
forward(200)
right(90)
```
